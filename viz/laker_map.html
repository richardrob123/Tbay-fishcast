Read this as a proxy, not a survey. The temperature comes from LSOFS (NOAA's Lake Superior model) with a provisional −3 °C correction — pending a Thunder Bay logger, the “GO” calls are directionally right but the exact distances can move. Bathymetry is coarse (a few soundings + a model anchor; MacKenzie’s slope is barely measured). LSOFS can’t resolve inside the cast zone, so this answers “is the cold water shallow & close today?” — not a metre-accurate inshore map. Real contour data drops straight in.

Shaded where the bottom is colder than each threshold within a ~75 m cast (≤22 m deep); nested, so darker = colder. Lakers prefer ~10 °C — 10–12 °C is the sweet spot, ≤8 °C marks strongest upwelling (not "colder is better"). The faint hatched zone is cold water just past a standard cast (a long cast or a short wade off a point) — the red line is the edge of easy-cast reach. Edges are uncertain ~100–300 m.

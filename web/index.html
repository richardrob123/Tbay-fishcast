🎣 Thunder Bay shore-fishing forecast

how to read this · ⓘ
Shaded where the bottom temperature is within the selected species' preferred range and reachable within a ~75 m cast (≤22 m deep). The bold edge is the thermal front — the temperature break where cold upwelled water meets warmer water and baitfish gather; that edge is where fish feed, especially as an upwelling relaxes (a fresh cold blast first slows the bite). Edges are uncertain ~100–300 m — treat them as fuzzy. Temperature is one cue: river mouths, points, break walls, and dawn/dusk matter too, and for salmon/steelhead the plume/season drives more than temperature. Forecasts water, not fish — check Ontario regs before fishing.
